What stays on your iPhone

Your saved conversations, memories, observations, practices and reflections are stored only on your iPhone, in a file iOS keeps encrypted whenever your iPhone is locked. Library bookmarks and reading positions also stay only on your iPhone. There is no sign-in, and nothing is synced between devices.

This storage is included in your iPhone backups (iCloud or computer), so restoring a backup to a new iPhone brings your history with it. Standard iCloud Backup is encrypted, but it is not end-to-end encrypted unless you turn on Advanced Data Protection. Without a backup, a lost or replaced iPhone means your history is gone, and we cannot restore it.

The things you type may reveal sensitive information, including your beliefs, health, feelings, or relationships. Share only what you want processed, and avoid including another person’s private information.

What our service keeps

When the app first connects, it creates an anonymous ID with no name, email or sign-in. Our service keeps only:

  • The anonymous ID, to recognise your app.
  • Your privacy choices: which AI permission you accepted, whether memory is on, and your language setting.
  • Daily usage counts for that ID, such as how many replies you used, to apply daily limits.
  • Purchases, if you buy a plan or messages: the App Store identifiers for your purchases and for this app on your Apple Account, which product you bought, when a subscription renews or ends, and how many messages you have left. Apple handles payment; we never see your card or Apple Account details.

We also keep monthly totals of AI calls, tokens and cost for the whole service, with no account identifiers. We do not keep your conversation text, memories, observations or practices.

We use this information only to provide the app, apply fair limits and keep the service working. We do not use it for advertising or sell it.

When AI receives your words

The app asks before enabling cloud AI processing. When you agree, each message and selected context from your iPhone (recent conversation and, if memory is on, relevant memories) travel through our service to Anthropic to generate a reply. With memory on, recent messages are also sent to suggest memories, which are then saved on your iPhone. Our service passes these requests along without keeping them, and it does not send Anthropic your anonymous ID.

You can withdraw AI permission in You → Privacy. This stops new AI processing; it does not erase earlier requests. Your saved conversations stay readable on your iPhone.

Temporary does not mean offline. A temporary chat is not saved at all, on your iPhone or by us, but each message still passes through our service and Anthropic. This service is not end-to-end encrypted.

Anthropic’s policy for its API says inputs and outputs are deleted within 30 days. Exceptions include legal requirements and enforcing its usage policy: content its automated safety systems flag as a policy violation may be kept for up to 2 years. We do not promise zero retention. See Anthropic’s retention policy for current details.

Manage what is remembered

  • Memories: inspect, correct, or forget a detail in You → Memories.
  • Memory switch: turn long-term memory off in You → Privacy to stop learning and using details across conversations. Existing memories remain on your iPhone for you to manage.
  • Individual conversations: exclude a conversation from personalization or delete it using its conversation options.
  • Temporary chat: use a conversation that is not saved or added to memory.

Forgetting a memory does not remove the words from an earlier saved conversation. Delete that conversation separately if you want to remove its transcript.

Erasing your data

Open You → Privacy → Erase all data. This erases every conversation, memory, observation and practice on this iPhone straight away, even without a connection. It also deletes the anonymous ID, your privacy choices and usage counts from our service as soon as it can be reached; if your iPhone is offline at the time, erase again once it is connected.

Deleting the app removes the saved data on this iPhone, but the anonymous ID can remain in your iPhone’s keychain and on our service. Use Erase all data first if you want both gone.

Erasing does not cancel a subscription or remove what you bought. Purchase records stay linked to the App Store identifiers for your Apple Account, so your plan and remaining messages come back after you erase or reinstall. Cancel a subscription in your iPhone’s Settings. To have purchase records deleted, contact us. Copies in your own earlier iPhone backups remain until those backups are replaced. Anthropic keeps requests it processed under its own policy, described above.

Earlier beta builds saved conversations on our service. That stored content was deleted on November 24, 2026.

Support messages and information you choose to send by email are handled in our support mailbox to respond to your request. Do not email passwords, sign-in codes, or a complete private conversation history.

Providers and security

Supabase hosts our anonymous sign-in service, small database and the functions that relay AI requests. Anthropic provides AI processing. Apple provides App Store and TestFlight distribution and processes payments for plans and message packs. Our database is configured in the United States; service providers may process information in other countries under their own arrangements.

Network requests use encrypted transport. Access controls separate each anonymous ID’s records. No security measure can guarantee complete protection.

The app has no advertising or cross-app tracking SDK. Service diagnostics can include the request route, response status and timing, without conversation text. Hosting and authentication providers may separately record network and security information, such as IP addresses. Apple may share TestFlight diagnostics and feedback according to your TestFlight settings.

For more information, see Supabase’s privacy policy, Anthropic’s privacy policy, and Apple’s TestFlight privacy information.

Beta access and children

The current beta is intended for invited development testers. Public access, including an experience for children, has not launched. The beta has no child accounts or parental-consent flow.

App Store distribution in European Union countries is not planned for this release. Store availability does not determine or waive any privacy rights that apply to you.

If a child has used the beta, the quickest way to remove what they saved is Erase all data on their iPhone. Contact us if you need help.
